Modifications (indication of changes, CC BY 4.0 section 3(a)(1)(B)):
- the sources were harmonised to one hourly schema (heat-pump electrical input, heat output where metered,
  outdoor, flow and return temperature), with new random household identifiers;
- location, coordinates, installer, floor area and property age were dropped at ingest;
- a conditional diffusion model was trained on the three sources to generate synthetic days; no generated day is
  in this release, because its memorisation audit failed (audit/, DATA_PROTECTION.md);
- data/aggregates_*.csv hold statistics over at least 50 households (HEAPO and heatpumpmonitor.org only);
- electrical input is expressed per household scale (see README.md); units and time are harmonised.
The custodians did not take part in this work and do not endorse it.

The simulation-pretrained baseline of the companion evaluation used RESCAST-100K
(https://huggingface.co/datasets/Jainam03/RESCAST-100k, CC BY 4.0); none of its data is in this release.
